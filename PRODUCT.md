# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro, chosen for a static-first, fast-loading marketing site that stays simple to maintain.

## Users

People in La Serena, Coquimbo, and the wider Región de Coquimbo who need a home appliance repaired or maintained, especially a washing machine.

## Product Purpose

Help a person with a broken appliance quickly understand what Lavaserv repairs, where it operates, and how to coordinate a home visit. Success means a qualified visitor starts a WhatsApp conversation or calls.

## Positioning

Local, human technical service with clear coordination by WhatsApp and a focus on getting essential household appliances operating again.

## Operating Context

The visitor is usually dealing with a time-sensitive household interruption on a mobile device. The current service workflow starts with a WhatsApp message or phone call, followed by scheduling a home visit and an on-site diagnosis.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary service: washing machine repair and maintenance.
- Additional services: dryers, dishwashers, kitchens, and other white goods when availability is confirmed.
- Current contact channels: WhatsApp and phone at +56 9 9989 9870; email contacto@lavaserv.cl.
- Coverage communicated by the current site: La Serena and Coquimbo, Región de Coquimbo.
- Conversion events should remain easy to wire to Google Ads and analytics.
- Pricing, response-time guarantees, technician names, and payment methods are open decisions and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Lavaserv name and the existing blue palette as the continuity bridge. The replacement should preserve the business's direct, service-oriented Spanish language while replacing the old WordPress layout.

## Evidence on Hand

The current public site at https://lavaserv.cl/ provides the existing service list, contact details, coverage, and WhatsApp links. No verified testimonials, pricing, staff profiles, or case-study assets were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make the next action obvious within seconds.
- Put washing-machine service first without hiding the broader line-white offering.
- Explain the visit in plain language before asking for contact.
- Keep claims grounded in the current business information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, and direct phone/WhatsApp alternatives for visitors who do not use one channel.
